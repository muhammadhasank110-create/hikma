## Browser speech-boundary resilience session (2026-08-13)

The initial mitigation for browser engines that omit later word boundaries used a guarded elapsed-time fallback. ## Audio-position synchronization correction (2026-08-13)

Estimated highlight cadence and duration-ratio word selection were removed because neither represents the actual spoken position. The capability probe now checks that timestamped synthesis is available, rather than merely checking ordinary audio generation. ## Live browser narration movement correction (2026-08-13)
